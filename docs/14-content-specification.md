# 2. Governing Content Principle

Every page must justify its existence.

A page should not be created merely because:

* a keyword exists
* a service/location relationship exists
* a competitor has a similar page
* a page can be programmatically generated
* the geographic registry contains the location
* the service × location matrix contains the combination

Every indexable page must provide:

```text
Distinct Search Intent
+
Distinct User Value
+
Accurate Business Relevance
+
Sufficient Unique Content
+
Clear Conversion Purpose
```

If these conditions cannot be satisfied, the page should not be published as an indexable route.

# 5. Primary Differentiator

The most important content differentiator is:

> **Independent sewer inspection and cleaning without repair-driven upselling.**

The website should consistently communicate that The Sewer Pros is focused on understanding and documenting the sewer problem rather than using inspection as an automatic path toward selling a major repair.

Appropriate concepts include:

* independent sewer inspection
* objective sewer diagnostics
* camera evidence before major decisions
* understanding the condition of the sewer line
* getting a second opinion before approving a major repair
* determining whether cleaning may resolve the issue
* separating diagnosis from repair sales

This positioning should be woven naturally into relevant pages.

It should not be repeated mechanically on every page.

# 6. Repair and Replacement Guardrail

The Sewer Pros must not be presented as a sewer repair or sewer replacement contractor unless specifically approved.

Do not imply that The Sewer Pros directly provides:

* sewer excavation
* sewer replacement
* sewer lining
* pipe bursting
* trenchless replacement
* structural sewer repair
* sewer installation

unless the business explicitly approves that capability.

Repair terminology may be used in educational contexts such as:

* signs that repair may be necessary
* deciding between cleaning and repair
* getting an inspection before replacement
* understanding structural sewer defects
* evaluating a repair recommendation
* obtaining an independent second opinion

The distinction must remain clear:

```text
The Sewer Pros
Diagnoses
Inspects
Documents
Locates
Cleans
```

versus:

```text
Repair Contractor
Repairs
Replaces
Excavates
Relines
```

unless an approved change expands the company's actual service scope.

# 10. Communication Style

Prefer straightforward statements such as:

> A sewer camera inspection allows the technician to see conditions inside the line instead of guessing based only on symptoms.

Avoid inflated language such as:

> Our revolutionary state-of-the-art advanced technology provides an unparalleled comprehensive solution for all your sewer needs.

Prefer:

> If a line keeps backing up after repeated clearing, a camera inspection can help determine why the problem keeps returning.

Avoid:

> Don't wait until disaster strikes!

# 18. AEO Answer-First Structure

Where a page answers an identifiable question, provide a direct response near the beginning of the relevant section.

Example:

## What does a sewer camera inspection show?

> A sewer camera inspection can reveal blockages, root intrusion, cracks, separated joints, offsets, pipe deterioration, standing water, and other visible conditions inside an accessible sewer line.

The direct answer can then be expanded.

This structure supports:

* readers
* featured snippets
* People Also Ask
* AI-generated answers
* LLM retrieval
* voice search
* structured content extraction

# 19. FAQ Standard

FAQs should address genuine questions associated with the page's search intent.

They should not exist only for keyword placement.

Good questions include:

* How does a sewer camera inspection work?
* What can a sewer camera see?
* Should I have the sewer inspected before buying a home?
* Can a sewer line be cleaned instead of replaced?
* Do you provide the inspection video?
* When should hydro jetting be considered?
* Can you locate where the sewer line runs?
* What causes recurring sewer backups?

Answers should generally begin with the answer itself.

Avoid opening every FAQ with:

> It depends.

Instead:

> Yes, in many cases a sewer camera inspection is worth performing before buying an older property because it can reveal conditions that are not visible during a standard home inspection.

Then provide qualifications.

---

# 20. FAQ Duplication Rule

Do not place the exact same FAQ block on hundreds of pages.

Localized or audience-specific pages should include questions relevant to that page.

Some foundational questions may naturally overlap, but the complete FAQ set should not simply be copied across every route.

# 55. Comparison Content Neutrality

Comparison pages should not manipulate readers by artificially making The Sewer Pros' option win.

For example:

> Hydro jetting is always better than snaking.

is inappropriate.

> Hydro jetting and mechanical drain cleaning solve different problems. The appropriate method depends on the type of blockage, pipe condition, line access, and the objective of the service.

# 79. Local Content Quality Test

A location-specific section should ideally fail the following substitution test:

# 80. Service Content Quality Test

A service page should fail the following substitution test:

> Could we replace "sewer camera inspection" with "hydro jetting" and leave most of the page unchanged?

If yes, the content is too generic.

---

# 81. Audience Content Quality Test

An audience page should fail:

> Could "home buyers" be replaced with "property managers" without substantially changing the content?

If yes, the audience page does not contain enough audience-specific value.

# 82. Commercial Content Quality Test

A commercial page should fail:

If yes, commercial differentiation is insufficient.

# 84. Minimum Unique Value Principle

There is no universal word-count threshold that makes a page valuable.

A 700-word page can be thin.

A 500-word page can be useful.

A 2,000-word page can be repetitive.

Content depth should match:

* user intent
* complexity
* competitive environment
* business relevance
* information available

The objective is **complete intent satisfaction**, not arbitrary length.

---

# 85. Content Depth Guidance

While no rigid word count should govern production, page families will often require different levels of depth.

### Core Service Pages

Generally substantial, authoritative coverage.

### Major Market Hubs

Substantial market-specific coverage.

### Service + Market Pages

Focused but complete local commercial intent.

### Audience Pages

Enough depth to address distinct audience concerns.

### Resource Articles

As long as necessary to answer the question well.

### Comparison Pages

Enough depth to explain actual distinctions objectively.

Do not pad pages to reach a target length.
